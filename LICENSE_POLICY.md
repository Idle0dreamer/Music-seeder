# Licensing policy

Music-seeder uses licenses by responsibility. `REUSE.toml` is the
machine-readable authority for the files currently in the repository.

## Code

The following are licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0:

- `src/`, `include/`, `apps/`, and `tests/`;
- future code under `tools/`;
- build and repository-operation files such as `Makefile`, `.gitignore`, and
  `REUSE.toml`.

The exact terms are in `LICENSES/MPL-2.0.txt`. The root `LICENSE` is retained as
the same MPL-2.0 text.

## Written work

The following are licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:

- `theory/`, the normative specification;
- the current Markdown-only `synthesis/` architecture and research notes;
- `README.md`, `STATUS.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and the licensing guidance;
- future original documentation under `docs/` and specifications under
  `specs/`;
- original, rights-cleared material under `profiles/`.

The exact terms are in `LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`. Commercial reuse and
adaptation are permitted subject to attribution and the license conditions.
Distributed adaptations must use CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible license
designated by Creative Commons.

Future synthesis implementation belongs under `include/mq/synthesis/`,
`src/synthesis/`, and `tests/synthesis/`, where the code classification above
applies. This keeps acoustic implementation separate from the theory and
musical-language specification.

## Profile boundary

CC BY-SA applies only to profile expression that a contributor has the right
to license. It does not override third-party copyright, performer or
repertoire rights, contractual limits, source terms, attribution duties, or
community protocols.

A contributed profile must identify its sources, provenance, rights basis,
and known restrictions. Do not copy or relicense a transcription, recording,
edition, corpus, or community-controlled material merely because it informs a
profile. References and factual observations are not a blanket license to the
referenced work.

## Copyright boundary

These licenses grant rights in code and other copyrightable expression. They
do not assert ownership over underlying ideas, algorithms, systems, methods,
procedures, facts, musical principles, maqamat, ajnas, sayr, intonation
practices, or community traditions. Whether a particular element is protected
depends on applicable law and the element itself.

This policy describes the project's intended licensing map; it is not legal
advice.
