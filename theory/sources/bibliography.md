# Sources and use

The specification distinguishes descriptive evidence from engineering
proposals. A source does not automatically validate every formalization built
on top of it.

## Maqam, jins, and modulation

- Sami Abu Shumays, [Maqam Analysis: A
  Primer](https://www.maqamlessons.com/analysis/media/MaqamAnalysisAPrimer_2013WebFormat.pdf).
  Supports jins as more than interval content, jins baggage, tonicization
  levels, constrained networks, and regional/performer variation.
- MaqamWorld, [Arabic Maqam](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/maqam.php) and
  [Maqam Bayati](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/maqam/bayati.php).
  Pedagogical orientation for sayr, phrasing, modulation, and Bayati’s common
  secondary ajnas. Used as a starting reference, not a complete grammar.

## Phrase, form, and melodic vocabulary

- Mondher Ayari and Stephen McAdams,
  [Aural Analysis of Arabic Improvised Instrumental Music
  (Taqsim)](https://www.mcgill.ca/mpcl/files/mpcl/ayari_2003_musperc.pdf).
  Supports motif/cell recurrence, phrase hierarchy, syntactic expectation,
  cadence formulas, development, re-exposition, and return.
- Zaher Alkaei and Mats Küssner,
  [Taqsīm as a Creative Musical Process in Arabic
  Music](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.640409/full).
  Supports rule-governed but flexible free-rhythm improvisation, repertoire
  building blocks, and non-rigid large-scale form.
- Sami Abu Shumays,
  [The Fuzzy Boundaries of Intonation](https://www.maqamlessons.com/analysis/media/FuzzyBoundaries_MaqamIntonation2009.pdf).
  Supports melody-word vocabulary, ornament as a combinatorial layer, regional
  pitch disagreement, and intonation inseparable from melodic context.

## Rhythm

- MaqamWorld, [Arabic Iqa](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/iqaa.php).
  Supports cycles structured by Dum, Tak, and silence.
- Encyclopaedia Iranica, [Īqāʿ](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/iqa/).
  Historical background on rhythmic modes and long/short beat organization.

## Corpora and modeling

- Zaher Alkaei et al.,
  [AMICOR: A Large Corpus of Arabic Melodic Improvisation in Call and
  Response](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10579-026-09905-z).
  Supports phrase-response data modeling, maqam-isolated datasets, and caution
  that merging related datasets can favor shorter, less sophisticated patterns.

## Hammered dulcimer acoustics

- Rainer Reuter and Alexander Zietlow,
  [Investigation of the Acoustical Properties of a Hammered
  Dulcimer](https://pub.dega-akustik.de/DAGA_2010/data/articles/000215.pdf).
  Supports strike-position, angle, force, and velocity effects; coupled
  string–bridge–soundboard behavior; and decay as a central quality parameter.
- Federico Fontana et al.,
  [Physics-Based Sound Synthesis of the Hammered
  Dulcimer](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2023/DAFx23_paper_18.pdf).
  Supports nonlinear hammer/string contact, stiff-string modeling, variable
  strike position, and time-varying synthesis parameters.

## Status of formulas

The operator grammar, exact tonicization evidence system, regional patch
algebra, contextual intonation formulas, time warp, and acoustic rejection
metrics are engineering proposals derived from the documented phenomena. They
are marked analytical until corpus and practitioner validation pass.
