# Hijaz canonical package

The canonical Hijaz package follows the published family description: root
Jins Hijaz on the tonic, with either Jins Nahawand or Jins Rast on the fourth
degree.

Sources:

- [Maqam Hijaz](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/maqam/hijaz.php)
- [Jins Hijaz](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/jins/hijaz.php)
- [Jins Nahawand](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/jins/nahawand.php)
- [Jins Rast](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/jins/rast.php)

The two upper-jins choices remain distinct typed routes with their own
provenance. Regional and historical realizations are separate future profiles.
