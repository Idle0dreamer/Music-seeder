# Ajam canonical package

The canonical Ajam package follows the published family description: root
Jins Ajam on the tonic, with either Upper Ajam or Jins Nahawand on the fifth
degree.

Source: [Maqam Ajam](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/maqam/ajam.php).

The two upper-jins choices remain separate typed routes. Regional, historical,
and repertoire variants are separate future profiles.
