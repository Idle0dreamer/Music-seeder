# Kurd canonical package

The canonical Kurd package follows the published family description: root Jins
Kurd on the tonic, followed by Jins Nahawand on the fourth degree.

Sources:

- [Maqam Kurd](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/maqam/kurd.php)
- [Jins Kurd](https://maqamworld.com/en/jins/kurd.php)
- [Jins Nahawand](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/jins/nahawand.php)

The single upper-jins route is represented as a branch in the family package,
not as a scale alias. Regional and historical realizations are separate future
profiles.
