# Hierarchical musical units

## Unit hierarchy

```text
Performance
└── Section
    └── Phrase relation
        └── Phrase
            └── Motif occurrence
                └── Melodic cell
                    └── Tone/attack event
```

Units can overlap. An ornament may anticipate the next tone, and a pivot cell
may close one phrase while preparing another.

## Attack event

An attack records:

- intended pitch trajectory;
- onset and release;
- energy;
- articulation;
- execution intent;
- membership in an ornament;
- relation to previous musical event.

## Melodic cell

A cell is a small attested and reviewed chunk with:

- relational contour;
- anchor and goal roles;
- characteristic rhythm class;
- permissible ornaments;
- tonal/jins compatibility;
- allowed transformations;
- evidence.

It is closer to a “melody word” than to an arbitrary interval tuple.

## Motif family

A motif family contains a prototype or cluster of related cells and phrases.
Membership is defined through preserved anchors, contour, rhythmic identity,
cadential goal, and attested transformations.

## Phrase

A phrase is a rhetorical unit with:

- opening condition;
- one or more motif occurrences;
- tonal function;
- tension trajectory;
- cadence or continuation;
- breath/pause behavior;
- relationship to neighboring phrases.

## Section

A section develops one modal idea over multiple phrases. It may:

- prepare;
- expose;
- confirm;
- vary;
- expand;
- contrast;
- re-expose;
- return;
- conclude.

These are functions, not a mandatory universal sequence.

## Performance

A performance carries global obligations and memory:

- declared maqām and form;
- root identity;
- regional/repertoire profile;
- performer vocabulary;
- register envelope;
- prior sections and motifs;
- unresolved cadence and modulation obligations;
- interaction with audience or ensemble when modeled.
